### 1.4 Submittals

1.4.1 Submit a mix design for each concrete class at least 21 days before the first pour. Each mix design submittal shall include:

a. Strength class, exposure class and intended elements.
b. Constituent materials with sources: cement type and content, additions, aggregates (type, source, maximum size), admixtures.
c. Free water/cement ratio.
d. Target slump or consistence class.
e. Trial mix results, including 7-day **and** 28-day cube strengths (minimum 3 cubes per age).
f. A curing method statement for the elements concerned (see 3.6).

1.4.2 Incomplete submittals will be returned without review.

### 2.2 Concrete Classes

| Element                              | Strength class | Exposure class | Max free w/c | Min cement content |
|--------------------------------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|--------------------|
| Foundations and ground beams         | C32/40         | XC2            | 0.55         | 300 kg/m³          |
| Suspended slabs (Levels 1 to 6)      | **C40/50**     | XC3            | **0.45**     | **340 kg/m³**      |
| Columns and core walls               | C40/50         | XC3            | 0.45         | 340 kg/m³          |

2.2.2 Maximum aggregate size: 20 mm for all suspended slabs.
2.2.3 Consistence class for suspended slabs: S3 unless agreed otherwise with the Engineer.
2.2.4 Chloride content class: Cl 0.40.
2.2.5 Cement: CEM I or CEM II/A to BS EN 197-1. GGBS replacement permitted up to 35%.

### 3.6 Curing

3.6.1 Cure all suspended slabs for a minimum of **7 days** after placing.
3.6.2 Acceptable methods: (a) spray-applied curing compound with a minimum efficiency of 90%, or (b) wet hessian kept continuously damp and covered with polythene.
3.6.3 The curing method shall be stated in the mix design submittal and in the pour method statement. Pours shall not proceed without an accepted curing method.

### 3.8 Testing

3.8.1 Make one set of 3 cubes per 50 m³ or per pour, whichever is more frequent, tested at 7 and 28 days.

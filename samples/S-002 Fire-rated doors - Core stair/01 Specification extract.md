### 1.3 Performance Requirements

1.3.1 Fire resistance shall be as scheduled on the door schedule HQB-LHA-DS-001 and the fire strategy HQB-PYR-FS-002.

| Location                                   | Rating   | Notes                                  |
|--------------------------------------------|----------|----------------------------------------|
| Core stair doors (all levels)              | **FD60S**| Self-closing, smoke seals required     |
| Lift lobby doors                           | FD30S    | Self-closing, smoke seals required     |
| Riser cupboards                            | FD60     | Locked shut, signage "Fire door keep locked" |
| Apartment entrance doors                   | FD30S    | Self-closing                           |

1.3.2 "FD60S" means 60 minutes integrity when tested to BS EN 1634-1 (or BS 476-22), with cold smoke seals tested to BS EN 1634-3.

### 1.5 Submittals

1.5.1 For each doorset type submit:
a. Manufacturer's product data and doorset drawings.
b. **Third-party certification** (BM TRADA Q-Mark or Certifire) covering the complete doorset, including glazing and hardware, **and** the supporting test or assessment report reference.
c. Ironmongery schedule with finishes.
d. Glazing details including fire rating of vision panels.

### 2.4 Glazing

2.4.1 Vision panels in fire doorsets shall carry the same fire rating as the doorset (60 minutes for FD60S doors), installed with the glazing system covered by the doorset certificate.

### 2.6 Ironmongery

2.6.1 Door closers: overhead, to BS EN 1154, minimum power size 3 for stair core doors.
2.6.2 Hinges: 3 no. per leaf, grade 13 to BS EN 1935, CE marked.
2.6.3 Finish for all ironmongery: **satin stainless steel, grade 316 (SSS)** to match the common-area palette.
2.6.4 Signage: "Fire door keep shut" on both faces.
